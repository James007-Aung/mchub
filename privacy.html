Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 28 Nov 2026

MC Hub (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides free disaster alerts, Myanmar news, a map of community places, and ways to meet and chat with Myanmar people nearby. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Guest use: You can use alerts, news and places without an account. We create an anonymous user ID so the app works; it is not linked to your name, phone number or email.
  • Account Information: If you sign up to use Nearby or Chat, we collect your phone number (OTP sign-in) or, with Google sign-in, your Google account's name, email address and profile photo, plus a unique user ID generated by Firebase. Your phone number and email are never shown to other users.
  • Profile Data: You may add a display name, profile picture, or bio. These are visible to other users.
  • Nearby location: If you choose to appear in Nearby, we store your location rounded to the centre of a fixed grid square of about 1 km. Your exact GPS position is not stored, and you can turn Nearby off at any time.
  • Disaster alert settings: The Myanmar states or regions you choose, and, if you turn on alerts near you, your rough area (about 200 km). Your rough area is used only to subscribe your device to alerts for that area; your location is not saved.
  • Chat Messages: Messages exchanged within the app are stored securely and are anonymized if accounts are deleted.
  • Feedback and reviews: Feedback and reviews you choose to send us.
  • Notification token: A device token so we can send the notifications you turn on.
  • Device Information: We may collect device identifiers, crash logs, and app usage analytics.
  • Localization Preferences: Your language choice (English or Myanmar) is stored for app settings only.

2. How We Use Information

We use your information to:

  • Send disaster alerts for the areas you choose.
  • Show Myanmar news, nearby places, and community content.
  • Provide sign-in, profiles, Nearby, and messaging between users.
  • Improve app stability, safety, and performance.
  • Send notifications about disaster alerts you choose, daily news digests, chat messages, or community updates.

3. Third-Party Services

MC Hub integrates with trusted third parties, including Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging, App Check, Crashlytics, Analytics), Google Sign-In, and the Facebook SDK (included but disabled; no data sent unless login feature is enabled later). Disaster alert data comes from USGS, EMSC and GDACS; we send them nothing about you. News articles open from the publishers' own websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Our website (mymchub.com)

When you follow a link to our website, we read the campaign tags in that link (for example, which ad or partner page sent you) and pass them to Google Play when you tap the download button. This tells us which campaigns bring new users. It does not use cookies and does not identify you.

With your permission only, our website may also use cookies from Meta (Meta Pixel) and Google Analytics to measure visits and download-button taps, so we can improve our ads. We ask before setting these cookies, and the site works the same if you say no. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored securely. Local data (Hive cache) is stored only on your device, cleared upon logout or deletion. No data is sold or rented to third parties.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

You may delete your account at any time through the app or via our web portal. Deletion permanently removes your personal data; chat messages and feedback may remain visible to others but will be anonymized. Aggregated, non-identifiable data may be retained for analytics.

6. User Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your information. You can withdraw consent by deleting your account. Contact us for questions regarding data handling.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to children under 13. If we learn that we have collected personal information from a child, we will delete it immediately.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted at https://www.mymchub.com/policies.html#privacy.
