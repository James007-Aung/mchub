Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 28 Nov 2026

MC Hub (“we,” “our,” or “us”) provides free disaster alerts, Myanmar news, a map of community places, and ways to meet and chat with Myanmar people nearby. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Guest use: An anonymous user ID; no name, phone number or email.
  • Account Information: Phone number (OTP) or Google account name, email and photo, plus a Firebase user ID. Phone number and email are never shown to other users.
  • Profile Data: Name, photo, bio (optional, visible to others).
  • Nearby location: Rounded to a ~1 km grid square, only if you appear in Nearby. Exact GPS is not stored.
  • Disaster alert settings: Chosen Myanmar states/regions and, if on, your rough area (~200 km) to subscribe to alerts. Location is not saved.
  • Chat Messages: Stored securely; anonymized on account deletion.
  • Feedback and reviews: What you choose to send.
  • Device Info: Notification token, crash logs, identifiers, analytics.
  • Localization: Language preference (English/Myanmar).

2. How We Use Information

  • Send the disaster alerts you choose.
  • Show Myanmar news, nearby places and community content.
  • Provide sign-in, profiles, Nearby and messaging.
  • Improve app performance and security.
  • Send notifications you turn on (alerts, daily news digest, chats).

3. Third-Party Services

Includes Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage, Cloud Messaging, App Check, Crashlytics, Analytics) and Google Sign-In. Facebook SDK is bundled but disabled in production. Alert data comes from USGS, EMSC and GDACS; news articles open on publishers' own websites.

4. Data Storage & Security

Data encrypted in transit, stored securely. No sale of personal data.

5. Retention & Deletion

Users can delete accounts anytime; personal data is removed, messages anonymized.

6. Rights

Access, update, or delete your data. Withdraw consent by deleting account.

7. Children

No services for children under 13.
